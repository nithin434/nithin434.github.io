AI Voice Calling Agent_

AI systems · 2024 – 2025 · AIR Centre · by Nithin Jambula

A real-time voice agent that makes and answers phone calls, then listens, understands and replies like a person. Calls run through Twilio, live audio streams over LiveKit, and Cartesia gives the agent a natural voice. LangChain with RAG grounds every answer in the right documents, and every call is logged for review. Deployed for live product outreach and demoed end to end.

Built with

LangChain · RAG · Twilio · LiveKit · Cartesia
